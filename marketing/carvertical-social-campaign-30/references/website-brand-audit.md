# Brand & Website Audit: MojAutoDiler.com (sr) & carVertical Partnership

## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Context
This audit was conducted by directly inspecting the live production website **https://www.mojautodiler.com/sr** across desktop and mobile viewports, combined with analyzing official carVertical marketing collateral and affiliate guidelines.

The purpose of this audit is to establish authentic brand guidelines, exact color tokens, typography rules, messaging standards, and visual compliance for the **30-ad social media campaign (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok/Reels)** promoting carVertical vehicle-history reports with the exclusive discount code **MOJAUTODILER**.

---

## 2. Platform Visual Identity: MojAutoDiler.com

### A. Brand Positioning & Tone of Voice
- **Category:** Premium auto marketplace and aggregator in Serbia.
- **Tagline:** *"Manje rizika. VIŠE poverenja."* (Less risk. MORE trust.)
- **Sub-label:** `PREMIUM AUTO MARKET`
- **Tone of Voice:**
  - **Editorial & Institutional:** Polite, professional second-person plural (*"Pronađite vozilo koje vam odgovara"*, *"Kupujte sigurnije"*).
  - **Promotional / carVertical affiliate:** Direct, engaging second-person singular (*"Kupuješ polovan automobil? Proveri njegovu istoriju."*).
- **Script:** Exclusively **Serbian Latin script** (*Srpska latinica*). Diacritics (č, ć, ž, š, đ) used consistently.

### B. Color System & Design Tokens
- **MojAutoDiler Primary Gold / Ochre:** `#C59B4C` (RGB: 197, 155, 76)
  - Light Gold Highlight: `#E8C97A`
  - Deep Gold Accent: `#9A7A2E`
  - Soft Gold Surface Fill: `#FBF6E8` / `#FDFBF7`
- **Neutral Charcoal / Obsidian:** `#171717` / `#0F0F14` (Headings, primary high-contrast text)
- **Subdued Text / Muted Slate:** `#6B6B7C` / `#4B5563`
- **Card Surface & Border:**
  - Pure White: `#FFFFFF`
  - Light Divider / Border: `#E2E2EA` / `#E5E7EB`
  - Page Canvas: `#F7F7F9` / `#FAFAFA`

### C. Typography
- **Headings & Accents:** Geometric sans-serif with tight tracking (`font-extrabold` / `font-black`, 800–900 weight, -0.035em letter spacing).
- **Body & Information:** Modern clean grotesque sans-serif (`Inter` / `DM Sans` / `system-ui`).
- **Brand Wordmark:** `Moj Auto Diler` + `MOJAUTODILER.COM` subtext.

### D. Logo & Shield Mark
- **Icon:** Circular / hexagonal shield badge containing a clean white automotive front silhouette on a warm gold ground (`#C59B4C`).
- **Usage:** Prominently featured in header lockups, co-branded header tags, and partner badges.

## 5. Creative Strategy for 30 Social Media Ads

### Group A: Instagram Feed (10 Ads, 1080 × 1350 px, 4:5)
- Optimized for standard Instagram feed viewing.
- Balanced vertical compositions with high visual intrigue, editorial hierarchy, and clear one-tap CTAs.

### Group B: Facebook Feed (10 Ads, 1080 × 1350 px, 4:5)
- Focused on mobile Facebook newsfeed readability.
- Clear value proposition readable in under 2 seconds, high-trust partner lockups, and social proof elements.

### Group C: TikTok & Instagram Stories / Reels (10 Ads, 1080 × 1920 px, 9:16)
- Mobile-first full-screen vertical formats.
- Safe-zone optimized: all core copy, promo codes, and CTAs strictly placed within the vertical central band (Y: 260px – 1560px) to prevent obstruction by platform navigation, profile handles, and action buttons.

### Legal & Regulatory Safeguards
1. **No Absolute Guarantees:** Ads never claim that 100% of accidents are detected or that vehicle condition is guaranteed.
2. **Precise Phrasing:** Always uses accurate language: *„Proveri dostupnu istoriju vozila”*, *„Više informacija. Bolja odluka.”*
3. **Consistent Footnote:** Discreetly displays the approved source disclaimer on every creative asset.
