## 3. Key Refinements & Quality Corrections Applied

### A. Branding Hierarchy & MojAutoDiler Logo Reduction
- **Primary Brand:** `carVertical` is visually dominant across all 30 creatives, placed prominently in header positions (left or centered) with high contrast.
- **Secondary Partner:** The `MojAutoDiler` logo was reduced to **40–50% of its previous displayed size** (height: 22–26px) and styled with a discreet `PARTNER: MojAutoDiler.com` tag.
- **Co-Branded Creatives (FB_18, TT_09, IG_08):** Inverted previous hierarchy so `carVertical` acts as the lead brand, eliminating oversized partner emblems.

### B. Branding Collisions & Spacing
- **TT_05 Collision Fix:** Eliminated the tight juxtaposition of the carVertical logo and promo code box inside the offer card. The header now houses `carVertical` and the subtle partner badge with ample margins, allowing the discount card to feature clean, uncrowded typography.
- **Header Margins:** Established standardized safe margins (minimum 56–72px on feeds, 80px on stories) ensuring no text or logo collisions.

### C. Serbian Grammar, Diacritics & Line Wrapping
- **Diacritics Verification:** Complete and accurate diacritics (*č, ć, ž, š, đ*) applied across all headlines, body copy, and CTA buttons (*kilometražu, oštećenjima, prošlosti, odlučiš, platiš, savršeno, zaslužuje*).
- **Line Break Tuning:** Adjusted typography line breaks to prevent single orphan words on the final lines of headlines.

### D. Promo Code Uniformity
- All 30 creatives consistently feature the official code: **`MOJAUTODILER`**.
- Accompanied by prominent **`20% POPUSTA`** messaging and high-contrast CTA buttons.
