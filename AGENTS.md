# MojAutoDiler Editorial & Writing Standards

## Editorial Rules
- **Never use em dashes (`—`) in MojAutoDiler editorial content. Write in natural Serbian Latin, using conventional punctuation and varied sentence structure.**
- Replace em dashes with natural Serbian punctuation (commas, periods, colons, or parentheses) depending on sentence context.
- Write in an authentic, professional Serbian automotive journalist voice: direct, informative, practical, and engaging.
- Avoid repetitive AI-style expressions, generic introductions, exaggerated claims, and unnatural transitions.
- Maintain accurate terminology, market prices in EUR, Serbian Latin orthography (č, ć, dž, đ, š), and clean formatting.
